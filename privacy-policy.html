Privacy Policy for SendChat

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

SendChat ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and permissions when you use the SendChat mobile and desktop applications.

1. Overview: Zero Cloud & Local Peer-to-Peer Architecture

SendChat is designed with a strict offline-first, peer-to-peer (P2P) architecture. All messages, files, photos, and media transferred via SendChat are transmitted directly between your devices over your local area network (Wi-Fi or hotspot).

  • We do not operate any central cloud servers to route, store, or inspect your communications.
  • We do not collect, track, sell, or monetize your personal information.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

SendChat accesses only the minimum necessary information on your device solely to perform the functions you explicitly request:

  • Files and Media: When you choose to send a file, image, or document, SendChat reads that specific file from your device storage to transfer it to your paired recipient. Received files are saved to your designated downloads folder.
  • Device Name & Platform: To allow nearby devices on your local network to identify each other, SendChat broadcasts your chosen device name and device type (e.g., Android, Windows, macOS, Linux) via local mDNS/UDP discovery.
  • Local Message History: Your chat messages and transfer logs are stored locally on your device in a secure local database (SQLite). This data never leaves your device unless you transfer it to another paired device.

3. Device Permissions and Justification

To function on Android and desktop operating systems, SendChat may request the following permissions:

  • Local Network / Wi-Fi State / Change Multicast State: Required to discover other devices on the same Wi-Fi network using Multicast DNS (mDNS) and UDP broadcasts, and to establish direct TCP/IP connections.
  • Storage / Media Access: Required to select files to send and to save files received from paired devices.
  • Camera (Optional): Used exclusively to scan pairing QR codes displayed on another device for instant mutual authentication. Camera frames are processed locally in real time and are never stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: Required to notify you of incoming messages, file transfer requests, and completed downloads.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain active, high-speed file transfers in the background without being interrupted by the operating system.

4. Security and Encryption

All communications between devices are protected by strong end-to-end cryptographic protocols:

  • Key Exchange: Devices establish secure communication sessions using Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) key exchange.
  • Authenticated Encryption: All data packets, chat messages, and file streams are encrypted using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • Mutual Verification: Connections can be confirmed using a 6-digit confirmation PIN or QR code to prevent man-in-the-middle (MITM) attacks.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

SendChat does not share, disclose, or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, analytics, or social login providers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All data generated by SendChat (chat messages, paired device history, and transfer logs) is stored locally on your device.

  • You can delete individual messages or entire conversations directly within the app using the multi-select and delete features.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage via your device settings immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

SendChat does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because our application operates entirely offline and peer-to-peer without account registration, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
